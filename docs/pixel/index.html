01 / THE QUESTION THAT STARTED IT ALL

WHAT IF YOUR OS CARED FOR YOU?

We have powerful computers, yet too much of our attention can disappear into updates, settings, troubleshooting and wondering what a system is doing in the background. Technology should give us room to create—not become another thing to constantly manage.

ZORX began with a question: Can we build a Linux system that respects its owner's privacy, stays understandable, and takes care of routine work wherever possible?

The journey began under the name ZORX OS. Today that same engineering journey continues as Ziuf OS. The name evolved; the ambition and technical history did not start over.

THE IDEA

LESS LOOKING AFTER YOUR OS.
MORE LIVING WITH IT.

Not magic. Not a promise of zero maintenance. A design direction: make everyday computing quieter, clearer and more respectful of the person at the keyboard.

01 / LESS MAINTENANCE

LET THE SYSTEM DO ITS PART.

We aim to reduce unnecessary upkeep through sensible defaults, recovery-minded design and routine tasks that demand less attention—while keeping important choices in your hands.

02 / PRIVACY WITH PURPOSE

YOUR LIFE IS NOT TELEMETRY.

Ziuf is being designed around no automatic user-activity telemetry, local-first diagnostics and deliberate consent when information is shared. Privacy must be engineered and verified, not merely advertised.

03 / INDEPENDENT FOUNDATION

MORE THAN A NEW WALLPAPER.

Ziuf is building a controlled Linux foundation, package-management path and modular Wayland-first desktop—not simply changing the appearance of an existing distribution.

04 / EVIDENCE BEFORE HYPE

BUILD IT. TEST IT. PROVE IT.

Bootstrap tools, system layers, package signing and security foundations are tracked through milestones and validation gates. We want the work behind the pixels to be inspectable.

A playful mascot can make a system welcoming. Good engineering must make it trustworthy. We are pursuing both, and we'll separate completed work from future ideas along the way.

03 / WHY ZIUF, WHEN OTHER OS OPTIONS ALREADY EXIST?

NOT HERE TO WIN A LOGO CONTEST.

Windows, macOS and established Linux distributions serve millions of people and offer mature software ecosystems. We respect that. Ziuf is exploring a different balance: independence without needless complexity, privacy without constant suspicion, and a desktop with personality without sacrificing engineering discipline.

WHAT MAKES ZIUF INTERESTING?

Instead of asking you to switch because of a slogan, we want to earn your confidence through the choices behind the system:

  • A controlled, independently built Linux foundation
  • ZPM package architecture and verification work
  • A modular, Wayland-first desktop direction
  • Local-first privacy, transparent diagnostics and user control
  • A thoughtful experience with less routine maintenance

WHY NOT INSTALL IT TODAY?

Because the answer should be honest: Ziuf is not a finished daily-driver OS. There is no stable public installation release yet.

Our documented progress includes toolchain, core-system, packaging and security groundwork. Desktop integration, broad hardware support, reliability testing and release engineering still have milestones to pass.

For everyday use today, a mature OS remains the practical choice. Follow Ziuf if these principles describe the kind of OS you'd like to use in the future.

Why it deserves a closer look: not because we claim to be better than every alternative, but because we're making our design decisions and engineering journey visible. When Ziuf is ready, the results—not the marketing—should make the case.

05 / PLAYFUL ON THE SURFACE. SERIOUS UNDERNEATH.

GAMING_

Work matters. Play does too. We want Ziuf to grow into a Linux operating system where games feel at home rather than like an afterthought.

That means building toward a dependable graphics and input foundation, exploring established Linux game compatibility tools, and making performance, updates and troubleshooting understandable—not overwhelming.

The OS is still under development. Gaming support will need real hardware, driver, controller and game-by-game tests before we can promise what works.

WHAT WOULD MAKE ZIUF WORTH GAMING ON?

MORE PLAY. LESS FRICTION.

We are not claiming more frames per second than Windows or flawless compatibility. Our ambition is a clean, responsive Linux gaming experience that puts the player in control. These are engineering targets—not features certified today.

01 / GRAPHICS FIRST

Establish reliable hardware acceleration, graphics drivers and the Vulkan/Mesa ecosystem where supported. Measure performance and stability on real GPUs before drawing conclusions.

02 / GAME COMPATIBILITY

Evaluate native Linux games and established compatibility paths such as Steam/Proton or Wine when the platform is ready. Some games, especially those with certain anti-cheat systems, may remain unsupported.

03 / QUIET & CONTROLLED

A modular desktop, sensible background behaviour, clear diagnostics and controller/input support are goals to validate—so the system supports your play instead of demanding constant attention.

Important: ZORX Blocks runs as ordinary JavaScript in your browser. It demonstrates our website's playful design, not gaming compatibility, speed or readiness of Ziuf OS. Ziuf has no stable public release yet.
